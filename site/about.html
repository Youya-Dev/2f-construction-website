Why use one firm for the whole job

Most renovations go wrong in the gaps between trades. We close those gaps.

  • One point of contact

    You deal with the same person from first visit to final walk-round. No chasing three different trades.

  • Fixed written quotes

    Every price is itemised in writing before work starts. If something changes, you agree it first.

  • Turning up when we say

    A start date and a programme of works you can plan around, with updates if anything moves.

  • Tidy every day

    Dust sheets down, rubbish cleared and tools put away, so you can still live in your home.
