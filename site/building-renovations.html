What we take on

Typical renovation work for homeowners and landlords across the city.

  • Knock-throughs and open-plan layouts

    Removing walls to join kitchens, dining and living spaces, including temporary supports and making good.

  • Structural alterations

    Steel beams and lintels fitted to an engineer’s design, with building control sign-off arranged.

  • Kitchen refits

    Strip-out, first fix, fitting and finishing, with joinery and decorating handled by the same team.

  • Bathroom refits

    Full replacements including tiling, boxing-in and any changes to the room layout.

  • Plastering and repairs

    Re-skimming, patch repairs and rendering, ready for decoration.

  • Whole-house refurbishments

    Bringing older or tired properties up to date, room by room or all at once.

Renovation questions

See all FAQs.

Will I need building regulations approval?

Structural changes, such as removing a load-bearing wall, usually need building regulations sign-off. We’ll tell you what applies and help arrange it.

Can we stay in the house while you work?

Usually, yes. We plan the work so you keep a working kitchen or bathroom for as long as possible, and we clean up at the end of every day.
