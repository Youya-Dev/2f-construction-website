Renovation questions

See all FAQs.

Will I need building regulations approval?

Structural changes, such as removing a load-bearing wall, usually need building regulations sign-off. We’ll tell you what applies and help arrange it.

Can we stay in the house while you work?

Usually, yes. We plan the work so you keep a working kitchen or bathroom for as long as possible, and we clean up at the end of every day.
