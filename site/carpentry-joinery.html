What we take on

Single jobs or full fit-outs as part of a renovation.

  • Staircases and balustrades

    New staircases, replacement treads and spindles, and handrail upgrades.

  • Doors

    Hanging internal and external doors, fire doors, and adjusting doors that stick or drop.

  • Fitted wardrobes and alcove units

    Made-to-measure storage, shelving and cupboards, ready to paint or finished in timber.

  • Skirting, architrave and panelling

    Period and modern profiles, wall panelling and window boards.

  • Flooring

    Solid timber, engineered and laminate floors, including levelling and thresholds.

  • First fix carpentry

    Stud walls, floor joists and roof timbers as part of building work.
