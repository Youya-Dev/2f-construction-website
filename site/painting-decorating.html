Painting and decorating in Nottingham

What we take on

Single rooms, whole houses, and rentals between tenants.

  • Interior painting

    Walls, ceilings, doors, skirting and woodwork, with furniture and floors covered throughout.

  • Exterior painting

    Masonry, render, fascias, soffits, windows and doors, using weather-resistant paints.

  • Wallpaper hanging

    Lining paper, feature walls and patterned papers matched cleanly at every seam.

  • Preparation and repairs

    Filling cracks, sanding, stripping old paper and minor plaster repairs.

  • Spraying

    A smooth factory-style finish for kitchen units, doors and joinery.

  • Landlord and end-of-tenancy work

    Fast turnarounds to get properties ready for the next tenant.
