Quotes and booking

Do you charge for quotes?

No. Home visits and written quotes are free, with no obligation to go ahead.

How soon can you start?

It depends on the time of year and the size of the job. We’ll give you a realistic start date with your quote.

Can you take on just one part of a job?

Yes. Plenty of customers only need decorating or a single piece of joinery. You don’t need to book the whole team.

During the work

Can we stay in the house while you work?

Usually, yes. We plan the work so you keep a working kitchen or bathroom for as long as possible, and we clean up at the end of every day.

Will I need planning permission or building regulations approval?

Most internal renovations don’t need planning permission, but structural changes usually need building regulations sign-off. We’ll tell you what applies and help arrange it.

What happens if the job changes once you’ve started?

We’ll explain the change and give you a price for it in writing. Nothing extra goes ahead until you’ve agreed it.
