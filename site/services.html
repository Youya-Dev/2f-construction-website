Building renovations

Full and partial refurbishments for houses and flats, from opening up rooms to bringing a tired property back to life. We manage the building work and the finishing trades together, so the job runs in the right order.

  • Knock-throughs and open-plan layouts
  • Structural alterations and steel beams
  • Kitchen refits
  • Bathroom refits
  • Plastering, rendering and repairs
  • Whole-house refurbishments

Carpentry and joinery

First and second fix carpentry, plus made-to-measure joinery built for your rooms, alcoves and awkward corners rather than a catalogue.

  • Staircases and balustrades
  • Internal, external and fire doors
  • Fitted wardrobes and alcove units
  • Skirting, architrave and panelling
  • Timber and laminate flooring
  • Stud walls, joists and roof timbers

Painting and decorating

Most of a good finish happens before the paint goes on. We fill, sand and prepare properly, cover your floors and furniture, then leave clean lines and even coats.

  • Interior walls, ceilings and woodwork
  • Exterior masonry, fascias and windows
  • Wallpaper hanging
  • Filling, sanding and surface repairs
  • Spraying for kitchens and doors
  • Landlord and end-of-tenancy work

How a job runs

The same four steps, whether it’s a single room or the whole house.

  1. Free home visit

    We come out, look at the space, and talk through what you want and what it’s likely to involve.

  2. Written quote

    Within a few days you get an itemised, fixed price and a proposed start date.

  3. The work

    We carry out each stage in order, keeping you updated and the site tidy.

  4. Walk-round and sign-off

    We go through every finished detail with you and put right anything you’re not happy with.
